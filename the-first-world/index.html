The intro

Before the first world, there was nothing. Then a light, then a voice, and then an island.

What it is

A god game where the island remembers.

You're a young god with no body, only a hand. One small island, a few villages that don't know you yet, and all the time in the world. Lumi and Fizz keep you company along the way.

Answer their prayers. Carry the lost child home. Drop a boulder where they're dreaming of a temple. Look after the place and it sings back to you. Get careless, and something out at the edge of the sea starts paying attention.

In the last age, the three villages raise the Nexus: seven marked standing stones. Telor grows around them, with warm windows and streets full of people.

Stay on the island and the seasons bring festivals, lanterns, dancing and Midwinter fireworks. Boats bring new families. Keep caring for Telor and it grows. There is no rush.

  • YOUR HAND

    Pick up almost anything: trees, boulders, people, the rain. Set it down gently, or throw it. Everything you touch leaves a mark.

  • YOUR PEOPLE

    Villages come to believe in you family by family. Their totem becomes standing stones, then a pyramid, then a temple, built where they dance for you.

  • ORA

    A round light that finds you at the big moments. It asks, it warns once, it notices. It never tells you what to do.

  • YOUR SHADOW

    Something waits beneath the sea. When your first pyramid rises, so does he, and every age you raise, his citadel grows too. He wants the same world you do, without the balance.

The split

Two voices. One world.

The voice of balance

ORA

A perfectly round light that met you before the island existed. It turns up at the big moments, asks you a question, and leaves the choosing to you.

The rival across the sea

VEXAHL

A shadow of smoke and embers who rises as you rise. Now and then he offers you his ring. Take it, and they kneel. Before the last age, he has something to say. The island is the struggle; this is a story about healing.

Lean too far either way and the island feels it. The road is about balance.
