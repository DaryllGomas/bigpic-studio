What it is

A tether on one arm, one shot in the other.

A fast capture-the-flag arena for the browser, built around two tools: a grappling tether on your left arm and a one-shot rifle in your right. Shrink down to mouse size in The Operator’s Study, a radio operator’s house where the desk is a plateau, the bookcases are cliffs and the real routes run behind the walls. Play against bots on Easy, Normal or Hard. Controller and keyboard supported.

  • The Operator’s Study: three rooms, at mouse size
  • Amber against cyan, first to three captures
  • Bots on Easy, Normal or Hard
  • Five ways between the bases in the study
  • Plays in the browser, nothing to install

In tribute

Richard “Levelord” Gray

Signal Arena was inspired by Richard “Levelord” Gray and the capture-the-flag nights of SiN, and made in tribute to him. The Operator’s Study is dedicated to his memory, 1957–2026.
